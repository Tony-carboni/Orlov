Capsuleers of The Orlov Family,

Two weeks ago I wrote that we were exactly where we set out to be. Today I can say we are ahead of it. The corporation stands at 60 pilots, the evenings on comms are busier than ever, and the things we do together have grown well beyond mining fleets. This message is about that growth, about the structure we are putting in place to carry it, and about some honest news on the alliance side.

## Where we stand

We are still growing rapidly, beyond our targets, and that includes the part that matters most: participation. The evening voice channel keeps filling up, and the people in it are doing more varied things than a month ago.

Our access to industrial infrastructure has expanded, and our range of activities with it. Where we first saw mostly mining fleets, we have since seen gas huffing, faction warfare activity, gatecamps hosted by our own people, duo abyssal runs and more. The broader scope, and the move toward activities with a different risk and reward, is exactly what the 60-day ambitions in the last message asked for. This is the right direction, and we keep going.

To carry that growth we are rolling out the first iteration of our leadership structure. More on that below.

We did lose two corporations from the alliance. That deserves its own section, further down.

## The last 30 days

- Monolithic Juggernaut [MONO1] joined the alliance on 18 October, 16 pilots strong.
- Content widened: gas huffing, faction warfare, gatecamps, duo abyssals, next to the mining fleets that keep the buyback fed.
- The buyback kept running at pace: 1.45 B ISK paid out to members through corporation projects since the budget baseline of 18 October.
- The first officers were named, and the officers' and directors' channels on Discord are live.
- Discord grew a leaderboard: the top pilots by project payouts over the last 30 days, refreshed daily. Alts registered in auth count for their main.
- Two public moons went on the moon board on a weekly timer: Raussinen on Wednesdays at 17:00, Piekura on Mondays at 18:00 EVE.

## The next 90 days

- **Keep recruiting.** The target stays one new member a day.
- **More fleets, across more of space.** From mining to day-tripping, from faction warfare to gatecamping, and everything in between.
- **More hosts.** We want more members hosting fleets, taking action and creating content. The leadership positions are being filled to make that happen, not to replace it.

## The leadership structure

The general structure is simple: Member, Officer, Director.

- **Members.** Everyone in the corporation is encouraged to take part in content, and just as much to host and organise it. Members who gravitate toward certain activities, or toward leading, will be asked to become officers.
- **The FC role goes.** It was created to open up pings and channels on Discord to a few more people. From now on every member can send pings, because we want everyone to feel free to step up and host something. The FC role is therefore dissolved.
- **Officers** are the operational leadership team. They take very active roles in areas such as recruitment, industry and faction warfare, and they are the points of contact to help organise activities and give guidance.
- **Directors** are the corporate leaders: less focused on content and fleets, more on the corporation's growth and direction, strategy and diplomacy.

The first officers have been assigned, and more will follow. If you want to take up a role, in whatever area, speak up. We would rather have too many volunteers than too few.

## On people and numbers

Since the October message the corporation has grown from about 33 pilots to 60, which is better than one and a half a day. The alliance as a whole went from 46 to 74 characters, even after two corporations left, thanks to our own growth and the arrival of Monolithic Juggernaut. On the auth side 19 new accounts joined since mid October; 37 of our pilots now have their characters registered, and the gaps are being chased one by one. The balance between veterans and newer players still looks healthy, and the target of one new member a day stands.

## On ISK

The corporation's finances are healthy. Since the budget baseline of 18 October the corporation has made about 165 M ISK net income on 1.5 B ISK of sales, and its own funds stand at about 644 M ISK above where they started.

The buyback is the engine. Corporation projects have paid 2.16 B ISK to members since they began on 4 October, 1.45 B of it since 18 October, and the goods sold on at a margin of about 15 %. That margin is what pays for the rest. The loyalty point programme bought 172,000 LP from members since 18 October and converted most of it, at a margin of about 10 %. Structure income came to 219 M ISK since the baseline, with the Athanors in Piekura now the larger part of it, and the structures burn about 103 M ISK of fuel a month. In short: the buyback pays the members well, the corporation earns a sensible margin on it, and the structures pay for themselves with room to spare.

## The alliance

Some sad news on the alliance front. Two of our four corporations, Go Browns and Cartographers, have left the alliance, taking about 15 characters with them. In heartbeats that was three people, but people we knew well and who were very active. They parted on good terms, and we wish them the best.

It does highlight something: while the corporation is flourishing, the alliance is not, or not yet. Over the next period we will work out a strategy for the alliance and what the best path forward is. Until then no new corporations will be recruited into the alliance. The focus may move away from small and growing corporations toward fewer, more established ones. All options stay open while we evaluate them, and alliance recruitment is paused for now.

## What I am asking of you

Three things. Keep bringing people in; one a day is the target and you are the recruiters. Keep hosting: a mining fleet, a gatecamp, an abyssal pair, a trip into low-sec, anything that gets five people in space with a plan. And if you want a role, say so. The officer positions are open to anyone who wants to carry part of the load, and the directors will back you with whatever you need to make it happen.

To everyone who showed up these past weeks, mined a rock, huffed a cloud, held a gate or kept comms warm at night: thank you. The corporation is becoming what we hoped it would be. Let's keep going.

Fly safe, fly together.
