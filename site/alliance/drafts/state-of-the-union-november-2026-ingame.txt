State of the Union, November 2026 (in-game version; the full post is on alliance.orlovfamily.space)

Capsuleers of The Orlov Family,

Two weeks ago I wrote that we were exactly where we set out to be. Today we are ahead of it. The corporation stands at 60 pilots, the evenings on comms are busier than ever, and what we do together has grown well beyond mining fleets: gas huffing, faction warfare, duo abyssals, with gatecamps in the planning. This is the direction the last message asked for, and we keep going.

The last weeks in short:
- Monolithic Juggernaut [MONO1] joined the alliance, bringing infrastructure in NPC null we may soon put to use.
- 1.45 B ISK paid out to members through corporation projects since the last communication.
- The first officers were named: Braadslee and Logan Grishnar, more to follow.
- Access to 8 public moons across 2 systems; plans drawn for more corporate Athanors.

Leadership: Member, Officer, Director. Members take part, host and organise. Officers are the operational team for recruitment, industry, faction warfare and more, your points of contact to organise activities. Directors handle growth, direction, strategy and diplomacy. Newer players are as welcome as veterans to become officers, as long as the intent to help build and coordinate is there. Want a role? Speak up.

People: from about 33 pilots to 60 since the October message, better than one and a half a day. The target stays one new member a day.

ISK: the buyback is the engine. You sell your ore to the corp at a slight discount and save the sales tax; the corp sells in bulk at full price for roughly a 10 % margin, which pays the fuel bill of about 100 M a month. The Raitaru added about 120 M. Corporate balance 644 M, net income this period 284 M, 1.45 B paid out in buybacks, 172,000 LP processed.

The alliance: Go Browns and Cartographers have left, on good terms, and we wish them the best. While the corporation flourishes the alliance does not yet, so alliance recruitment is paused while we work out the best path forward.

What I ask of you:
- Keep bringing people in. One a day, and you are the recruiters.
- Keep hosting. Anything that gets people in space with a plan.
- If you want a role, say so. The directors will back you.

Thank you to everyone who showed up, mined a rock, huffed a cloud or kept comms warm. Let's keep going.

Fly safe, fly together.
Catherine Frey, Alliance Director
