# Runbook: The Blood-Stained Stars for Caldari standing (without hurting Gallente)

*For Catherine Frey, 2026-10-15. Goal: get Caldari State standing from −1.0 to above 0.0 so she can enlist in Caldari faction warfare, while leaving Gallente standing alone. The epic arc's final reward is a direct faction gain with no derived change to the other empires; a single Caldari storyline mission afterwards covers the last few hundredths. Pilot skills assumed excellent.*

## The plan in one line

Arc first (lands at about −0.04), then 16 quick Caldari missions and the storyline they trigger (crosses 0.0), then enlist. Order matters: the arc's gain is larger the lower your standing is, so run it before anything else.

## A. Before you undock (15 min)

- **Clone and implants.** Fly a clone without expensive implants; you will be in high-sec throughout, but the arc has you warp to many sites and one boss fight.
- **Ship.** A destroyer is enough; a Tactical Destroyer (Jackdaw, Hecate, Confessor, Svipul) is the comfortable choice with excellent skills: speed, range control, and the DPS the last fights need. Fit an **omni tank** (the arc throws Serpentis, Blood Raiders, rogue drones and mercenaries at you, in that mix) and bring the ammo or crystals for two damage types. A microwarpdrive helps with the travel-heavy sites.
- **Cargo.** At least **150 m³** free cargo for mission 19 "An Economy Under Threat"; a destroyer with an expander, or keep a hauler alt nearby. Several missions hand you small items to carry; do not jettison or sell them.
- **Spare high-DPS modules** in cargo or at a station on the route, for "Our Man Dagan" (see D).
- **Agent.** Sister Alitura, Sisters of EVE, station in **Arnon** (Essence, Gallente high-sec). No standing needed; the arc is level 1 content.
- **Not in a fleet.** NPC conversations in space do not appear while you are fleeted. Leave the fleet before you start.
- **Time.** Seven chapters, 52 missions, a few hours of mostly travel; it can be spread over days. Nothing expires quickly, but finish one chapter at a time so you do not lose the thread.

✅ Done when you sit in Arnon, docked, in the ship above, with the cargo space free.

## B. The arc, chapter by chapter

Accept each mission from the agent it comes from and follow the chain; the agent for the next mission is named in the completion text and shows in the journal.

| Chapter | Missions | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Quality of Mercy | 1–10 | Arnon and around: couriers and small fights, sets up the story |
| 2 Automaton Impediment | 11–15 | Rogue drones |
| 3 Shadow Puppets | 16–21 | Investigation; mission 19 needs the 150 m³ |
| 4 Queens and Drones | 22–29 | Rogue drone hives, a branch with three parts, then a shared finale |
| 5 Shifting Foundations | 30–37 | Blood Raider flavour |
| 6 A Breach of Trust | 38–44 | The betrayal; combat picks up |
| 7 Closing In | 45–52 | The chase, Dagan, and the faction choice |

Pace yourself in chapter 4: the hive missions are the busiest, many small drones; kill the ones that web or scram first and the rest is a grind.

✅ Done when the journal shows chapter 7 started.

## C. The choice that matters (chapter 7)

Near the end Sister Alitura offers **four "Commander" missions, one per empire**. Only one can be taken and it decides which faction gets the final standing. **Take the Caldari one.** Decline or ignore the Amarr, Gallente and Minmatar commanders; declining those has no standing effect.

✅ Done when the Caldari commander's mission is in your journal.

## D. Our Man Dagan (the one hard fight)

Dagan is a single boss with high resistances to every damage type and a solid tank; he does not hit hard, he just takes long to die. What wins is sustained DPS, not more tank:

- Refit for damage before the mission: damage mods in the lows, the best ammo or crystals you have, drones out if the hull carries them.
- Orbit at your optimal, keep applying, and bring enough ammo for a long fight; there is no resist hole worth planning around.
- If the DPS is not there, warp out, refit, come back; the site does not reset.

✅ Done when Dagan is dead and the final mission completes with the message about the Caldari State.

## E. After the arc

- Check the character sheet: Caldari State base standing should read about **−0.04** (from −1.0 with Social V). Gallente unchanged.
- Run **16 missions for a Caldari State corporation agent** (Caldari Navy, level 2 or 3 if you have the ship for it; distribution missions are quickest). The 16th triggers a **storyline** offer; accept it. One storyline pays a few hundredths at this level, enough to cross 0.0, and costs Gallente only a fraction of that.
- Check again: Caldari State above 0.0 (strictly above; the enlist screen's "0.0 or above" wording is wrong).
- **Enlist** in Caldari faction warfare from the militia office. Enlisting itself does not touch Gallente standing; what you shoot afterwards does.

✅ Done when the militia tab shows you enlisted.

## If something goes wrong

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| The arc agent will not talk to you | You ran it less than 90 days ago, or you are in a fleet. |
| "An Economy Under Threat" will not complete | Cargo too small: 150 m³ needed. |
| Dagan will not die | Refit for DPS, not tank; warp out and back is free. |
| Standing after the arc is still well below 0 | The gain was applied to a different faction: the wrong commander mission was taken. The arc can be repeated in 90 days. |
| Standing shows −0.0x after the storyline | Run 16 more missions for a second storyline, or wait for the 90-day repeat of the arc (lands around +0.8). |

Sources: EVE University, The Blood-Stained Stars and Faction warfare pages (checked 2026-10-15).
