# Runbook: The Blood-Stained Stars for Caldari standing (without hurting Gallente)

*For Catherine Frey, 2026-10-15, merged 2026-10-16. Two goals, one arc: get Caldari State standing above 0.0 to enlist in Caldari faction warfare, and lift it further over the year to lower the Jita broker fee. The arc's final reward is a direct faction gain with no derived change to the other empires. Pilot skills assumed excellent.*

## The plan in one line

Arc first (the gain is a share of the distance to 10.0, so the lower you start the more it gives), then 16 quick Caldari missions and the storyline they trigger if you still sit below 0.0, then enlist; repeat the arc every 90 days.

## Why, in your numbers

- **Faction warfare** needs Caldari State strictly above 0.0.
- **Jita broker fee** = 3 % − 0.3 pp per level of Broker Relations (you have V) − 0.03 pp per point of **Caldari State** faction standing − 0.02 pp per point of **Caldari Navy** corporation standing (the owner of Jita 4-4). The Shipyard reads your standings from EVE and uses exactly this (runbook 13 C, release 0.1.7).

| Standing of Catherine Frey (unmodified, read by the Shipyard 2026-10-14) | Now | After one arc (Social V) |
|---|---|---|
| Caldari State (faction) | **−0.27** | **≈ +0.63** |
| Caldari Navy (corporation) | +2.34 | +2.34 (the arc does not touch it) |
| Jita broker fee | 1.461 % | ≈ 1.434 % |

(If the character sheet shows a lower figure than −0.27, that is a different reading of the same standing; the arc gives +0.875 of the distance to 10.0 from wherever it starts: from −1.0 it lands at about −0.04, from −0.27 at about +0.63.)

Your wallet paid about 7.2 B in broker fees in the month to 2026-10-14, roughly 490 B of orders placed and relisted, so 0.027 pp less is about **130 M per month**. Four arcs a year take the standing to about +2.9, about 0.1 pp of fee, roughly 480 M per month at today's volume. The bigger step afterwards is **Penumbra** (section G).

## A. Before you undock (15 min)

- **Skills.** Social V is the one that matters: it turns the arc's +0.7 into **+0.875** of the remaining distance to 10.0. Connections and Diplomacy only change what agents see, not the stored gain.
- **Clone and implants.** Fly a clone without expensive implants; high-sec throughout, but many warps and one boss fight.
- **Ship.** A destroyer is enough; a Tactical Destroyer (Jackdaw, Hecate, Confessor, Svipul) is the comfortable choice: speed, range control, and the DPS the last fight needs. Fit an **omni tank** (Serpentis, Blood Raiders, rogue drones and mercenaries, in that mix) and bring ammo or crystals for two damage types. A microwarpdrive helps with the travel-heavy sites. A Caracal or a Gila also walks through it.
- **Cargo.** At least **150 m³** free for mission 19 "An Economy Under Threat"; a destroyer with an expander, or a hauler alt nearby. Several missions hand you small items to carry; do not jettison or sell them.
- **Spare high-DPS modules** in cargo or at a station on the route, for "Our Man Dagan" (section D); a mobile depot lets you refit in the site.
- **Agent.** Sister Alitura, Sisters of EVE, *Arnon IX - Moon 3 - Sisters of EVE Bureau* (Essence, 0.6, about 20 jumps from Jita). No standing needed; the arc is level 1 content.
- **Not in a fleet.** NPC conversations in space do not appear while you are fleeted. Leave the fleet before you start.
- **Mission queue.** Clear anything from Amarr, Gallente or Minmatar agents that would have you shoot Caldari ships; declining or failing those is what erodes empire standings, not this arc.
- **Time.** Seven chapters, 51 missions, a few hours of mostly travel; it can be spread over days. Nothing expires quickly, but finish one chapter at a time so you do not lose the thread.

✅ Done when you sit in Arnon, docked, in the ship above, with the cargo space free.

## B. The arc, chapter by chapter

Accept each mission from the agent it comes from and follow the chain; the agent for the next mission is named in the completion text and shows in the journal.

✅ Done when the journal shows chapter 7 started.

## C. The choice that matters (chapter 7)

Near the end Sister Alitura offers **four "Commander" missions, one per empire**. Only one can be taken and it decides which faction gets the final standing. **Take the Caldari one.** Decline or ignore the Amarr, Gallente and Minmatar commanders; declining those has no standing effect. If the last mission, *Dal Segno Al Fine*, offers a choice of reward as well, take the **Caldari State** one again.

✅ Done when the Caldari commander's mission is in your journal.

## D. Our Man Dagan (the one hard fight)

Dagan is a single boss with high resistances to every damage type and a solid tank; he does not hit hard, he just takes long to die. What wins is sustained DPS, not more tank:

- Refit for damage before the mission: damage mods in the lows, the best ammo or crystals you have, drones out if the hull carries them. 250 DPS kills him in a few minutes.
- Kill the escorts first, then orbit at your optimal, keep applying, and bring enough ammo for a long fight; he does not jam or neutralise, and there is no resist hole worth planning around.
- If the DPS is not there, warp out, refit, come back; the site does not reset.

✅ Done when Dagan is dead and the final mission completes with the message about the Caldari State.

## E. After the arc

- Check the character sheet: Caldari State should have gained **0.875 of the distance to 10.0**, Caldari State only, Gallente unchanged. The Shipyard picks the new value up with its daily refresh, or at once with **Refresh from EVE** on My settings; the broker fee on the dashboard drops with it.
- Still below 0.0? Run **16 missions for a Caldari State corporation agent** (Caldari Navy, level 2 or 3 if you have the ship; distribution missions are quickest). The 16th triggers a **storyline** offer; accept it. One storyline pays a few hundredths at this level, enough to cross 0.0, and costs Gallente only a fraction of that.
- Check again: Caldari State strictly above 0.0 (the enlist screen's "0.0 or above" wording is wrong).
- **Enlist** in Caldari faction warfare from the militia office. Enlisting itself does not touch Gallente standing; what you shoot afterwards does.

✅ Done when the militia tab shows you enlisted.

## F. Repeat every 90 days

The arc resets 90 days after you finished (or failed) it; Sister Alitura offers *A Beacon Beckons* again. Put the date in the calendar. Each run is worth 8.75 % of the distance to 10.0: from +0.63 the next run gives about +0.82, the one after about +0.75.

## G. The bigger step afterwards: Penumbra

The Caldari epic arc **Penumbra** (agent Aursa Kunivuri of Expert Distribution, Josameto, The Forge, two jumps from Jita) needs **5.00 standing with Caldari State, or with Expert Distribution, or with the agent** (effective standing, so Connections counts here). The fast lane is **Expert Distribution corporation standing**: distribution missions for any of its agents raise corporation standing per mission, and every 16th mission of a level offers a storyline that lifts Caldari State too (small derived loss to Gallente and Minmatar). A few evenings of level 3 or 4 hauling in a blockade runner reach 5.00 with the corporation.

Penumbra pays **10 % Caldari State** at the end of either path plus **10 % more for the closing mission "Home in Peace"**, each without derived losses, plus about 20 % Caldari Navy corporation standing (the Jita station owner, so the broker fee drops twice), ISK and a faction blueprint copy. Warnings from the wiki: at least one hauling mission to low or null security space, a route through Triglavian minor victory systems, known gankers around the heavy combat missions, a data analyzer needed for "Right Tool for the Job", a battleship or strategic cruiser for three of the missions. A separate runbook when you get there.

## If something goes wrong

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| The arc agent will not talk to you | You ran it less than 90 days ago, or you are in a fleet. |
| "An Economy Under Threat" will not complete | Cargo too small: 150 m³ needed. |
| Dagan will not die | Refit for DPS, not tank; warp out and back is free. |
| Standing after the arc is still well below 0 | The gain was applied to a different faction: the wrong commander was taken. The arc can be repeated in 90 days. |
| Standing shows −0.0x after the storyline | Run 16 more missions for a second storyline, or wait for the 90-day repeat of the arc. |
| Declined an agent twice in four hours | That costs standing with the agent's corporation and faction; the arc's own missions never need declining. |
